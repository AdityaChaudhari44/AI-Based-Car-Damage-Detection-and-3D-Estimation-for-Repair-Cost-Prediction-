AI-Based Car Damage Detection

Damage analysis, repair-cost estimation and nearby garage discovery

1. Analyze car damage

Upload a clear JPEG or PNG photo of the damaged vehicle. The app sends it to your local FastAPI analysis service.

2. 3D surface reconstruction

Creates an interactive 3D surface preview from the uploaded image's estimated relative depth. Red points/surface areas indicate the detected damage mask. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and use the Plotly toolbar to reset or download the view.

Approximate relative depth Interactive 3D mesh

Upload and analyze an image to generate the 3D surface preview.

Research limitation: A single RGB image cannot provide verified real-world dent depth in centimetres without calibration or additional views. This visualization is an approximate relative-depth surface, not a measurement-grade reconstruction.

Enter a city, area or full address, or allow browser location access. Google Maps will search for car repair garages near that location.
